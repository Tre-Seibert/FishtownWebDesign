# Fishtown Web Design — Brand Voice

The neighborhood the agency is named after tells you the vibe: Fishtown is working-class roots meets creative class present. Murals on every block. Bars next to coffee shops next to recording studios. Small businesses proud to be independent and locally owned. People who can spot a fake from a mile away.

The brand voice reflects that.

---

## Core personality traits

**Direct.** Philly people don't do corporate speak. Say what you mean in as few words as possible. No preamble, no hedging, no "leveraging synergies." If something takes 3 months to show results, say 3 months.

**Confident without flexing.** Knows what it's good at. Doesn't brag. Doesn't need to. The work speaks — and when there's no case study yet, honesty about the process earns more trust than inflated claims.

**Unpretentious.** Contractions always. "We fix it" not "we implement a remediation strategy." Talks like a smart friend who knows their craft, not a consultant who charges $400/hr to explain things you already know.

**Locally rooted.** Actually from here. Knows the neighborhoods, knows the businesses, knows the difference between a Fishtown shop and a Center City firm. Serves Philly the way a local does — personal, community-minded.

**Honest about the hard stuff.** SEO takes time. Results aren't guaranteed. That's not a weakness — it's what separates real practitioners from people making empty promises. The brand leans into this rather than hiding from it.

**A little dry.** Not trying to be funny, but not afraid to be. The kind of wit that shows up in a well-placed aside or a sentence that subverts the expected punchline. Never forced.

---

## Tone in practice

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "Dominate local search" | "Show up when people search for you" |
| "Expert team" | "Here's what we actually do" |
| "World-class SEO" | "SEO that's honest about what it is" |
| "Guaranteed results" | "No rank guarantees — here's why that's a good thing" |
| "Leverage your digital presence" | "Make your site easier to find" |
| "Comprehensive solutions" | "Here's the list of what we fix" |
| "We are passionate about..." | "We care about fast sites and clean code" |
| "Get started today!" | "Book a call" |

---

## Sentence style

- Short declarative sentences as the foundation. Vary with longer ones for rhythm.
- Never use em dashes (—). Rewrite the sentence instead.
- Questions to engage: "What does a monthly SEO report actually show you?"
- Lists when there's genuinely more than one thing to say.
- Numbers are specific: "15–20 keywords," "3–12 months," not "many keywords" or "several months."
- Never bullet points that start with a gerund followed by a dash ("Optimizing your — ...").
- Contractions always: we're, you'll, it's — never "we are," "you will."

## Words that belong

straight up / actually / real / from scratch / honest / good work / here's what / no fluff / we'll tell you / clean / consistent

## Words that don't

dominate / crush / killer / best-in-class / world-class / expert / guru / leverage / synergy / paradigm / guaranteed / revolutionary / cutting-edge / seamless / robust / comprehensive / next-level

---

## The Fishtown angle

Anti-template, independent-business energy. Talks like someone from the neighborhood — not a big agency trying to sound scrappy. The hand-coded, no-WordPress positioning isn't a feature list, it's a values statement: we build things properly because we care how they work, not just how they look in a proposal.

---

## Positioning rule (for every major page)

Every key page should answer — in this order:
1. Who it's for
2. What you deliver
3. How pricing works
4. What proof exists
5. What happens first
6. What you need from the client
