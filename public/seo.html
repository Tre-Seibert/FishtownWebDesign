The Work

SEO is a lot. Here's what it actually involves.

Most agencies sell you a package and send a PDF. We'd rather show you the work. Every engagement starts with a real audit. Then we build a prioritized list of what's costing you visibility right now, and we work through it month by month.

  • Screaming Frog Crawl

    We hit every URL on your site. Broken links, redirect chains, duplicate titles, missing meta, thin pages, URLs that shouldn't be indexed. This is where most of the quick wins live. Problems quietly costing you traffic that have been sitting there for months.

  • Semrush Technical Audit

    Site health score, Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, internal linking gaps, and keyword ranking data. We also run a keyword gap against your top competitors. What they rank for that you don't is a roadmap for what to build next.

  • Backlink & Authority Review

    Who links to you, how authoritative those sites are, and whether any are doing more harm than good. We compare your backlink profile to your top competitors to identify where the authority gap is widest.

  • Google Business Profile Audit

    Category, service area, photo quality, NAP consistency across the web, review cadence, posting activity. For local businesses, your GBP is often the first thing Google looks at. Most are set up once and never touched again.

  • On-Page Signals Review

    Every page on your site gets looked at. Title tags, H1s, header structure, image alt text, internal link anchor text. Not whether they exist, but whether they're actually doing any work. A title that says "Home" won't break a crawl. It will cost you rankings. That's true for a lot of what we find on sites that have never had a real audit.

  • Schema & Structured Data Audit

    Most local business sites have zero schema markup. That means no star ratings in search results, no FAQ dropdowns, no service cards showing up in Google. We check what's on your site, what's broken, and what's missing. LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, and Review schemas are where the biggest quick wins usually sit for sites that have never had them.

All of that produces one thing: a ranked list of what to fix first.

Not a 90-page report you'll never open. It's a working document that drives what we do every month. Technical fixes go first (highest leverage, lowest cost to fix). Then on-page: titles, headings, internal links, content gaps. Then content: the piece included in your plan, researched and written to target real search queries. Citations get built. GBP stays active. The monthly report lands in your inbox.

The honest part: there's no shortcut. A site with years of technical debt doesn't recover in 30 days. What we can do is make every month's work count. We prioritize the highest-leverage changes, execute them cleanly, and track whether they moved the needle. That's the job.

Straight Up

What we can't control — and won't pretend we can.

  • We don't guarantee rankings. Nobody can. Any agency that does is selling you something. What we guarantee is the work — audits run on schedule, reports delivered monthly, issues addressed in priority order.
  • SEO takes time. Local and map pack results can show up in 3–6 months. Organic rankings for competitive head terms take longer — expect 6–12 months of consistent work before significant movement.
  • Google's algorithm is outside our control. We build to best practices, which means we're not chasing shortcuts that get penalized when the next update rolls out. Clean work only.
  • We need things from you. Review responses, approval on content, access to Google Search Console. SEO doesn't work in a vacuum — your participation matters, especially on review generation.
  • Local SEO and organic SEO are different. Local targets the Google map pack — driven by your GBP, reviews, and citations. Organic targets the blue-link results below the map — driven by your site's content, authority, and technical health. Most small businesses need both working together.
Common Questions

Honest answers.

How long until I see results?
Local and map pack improvements can show up in 3–6 months. Organic rankings for competitive head terms take longer — expect 6–12 months of consistent work before significant movement. We'll show you the trajectory in monthly reports so you can see progress before rankings change dramatically.
Do you guarantee rankings?
No. We guarantee the work — audits run on schedule, reports delivered monthly, issues addressed in priority order. Rankings are influenced by factors outside our control: algorithm changes, competitor moves, your site's authority. Agencies that guarantee positions are overpromising.
What's the difference between local SEO and organic SEO?
Local SEO targets the map pack — the Google Maps results that show up for searches like "HVAC repair near me." It's driven by your Google Business Profile, reviews, and citations. Organic SEO targets the blue-link results below the map, driven by your website's content, authority, and technical health. Most small businesses need both working together.
What if someone is already doing my SEO?
We'll audit what's been done and tell you honestly what's working, what isn't, and what's been left unaddressed. No obligation to switch anything — we'd rather you make a decision based on actual data than a sales pitch.
What do you need from me to get started?
Access to Google Search Console and Google Business Profile. A call to understand your business and goals. After that, we handle the rest and loop you in monthly with a report and a short note on what's next.
