The vetting happens before you ever pick up the phone

A $15,000 job gets researched differently than a $200 drain call.

Most roofing jobs are the biggest home repair a homeowner makes in a decade. They're not calling the first result. They're looking at three or four sites, reading reviews, checking photos, confirming the company address is local. Fifteen minutes of research before a single phone call.

After a storm, that vetting gets even more intense. Storm chasers are real. Homeowners know it. An out-of-state truck in the driveway is a red flag now. Your website either confirms that you're the established local roofer or it leaves enough doubt that they keep scrolling.

Here's what they check before they call.

✓
Photos look like actual local jobs, not stock images of generic roofs
✓
Reviews are recent and mention specific job types (hail damage, full replacement)
✓
Site loads fast — stressed homeowners have no patience for a spinner
✓
Phone number is visible without scrolling
✓
Service area mentions their neighborhood or county
✓
Company looks established, not a crew that appeared after the storm

If your site fails two or three of these, they call someone else. They never tell you why.

Your portfolio is your proof

Your photos close the job. Most sites make them impossible to find.

Roofing is one of the most visual trades. A homeowner looking at worn shingles wants to see what a new roof looks like. Clean ridge caps, tight flashing, valleys done right. Your before-and-after photos are the closest thing to a guarantee you can offer before picking up the phone.

Most roofing sites either don't have real photos or they're stuffed into a gallery tab nobody opens. Or the images are stock photos that any homeowner can reverse-image-search in 10 seconds. Or they're large files that take 6 seconds to load on mobile and the homeowner gives up.

We build the site around your actual photos. Fast, properly sized, displayed where they do the work.

Common Questions from Roofers

Do I need a separate page for storm damage repair, or does one site cover everything?

One site covers everything if it's built right. You want a site that ranks for both "roofing contractor [your city]" and "storm damage roof repair [your city]." We build the content structure so both types of searches can find you. No separate site needed, just the right sections in the right places. See our web design services page if you want to see the full picture of what we build.

How do I make sure my site doesn't look like a storm-chaser operation to homeowners?

Three things matter: real photos of your local work, a local address or service area clearly stated, and recent reviews that mention specific job types. We build the site to surface all three above the fold. Homeowners making a $15,000 decision are looking for exactly this. A professional, fast-loading site is itself a signal that you're an established local business.

How do I rank for "roofing contractor [my city]" in local search?

A fast, well-structured site is the technical foundation. Your Google Business Profile does the local work. They have to work together: a site Google can crawl quickly and a GBP with consistent information and recent reviews. We handle the site side. See our SEO services if you want help with the ongoing local ranking piece.

Can you build a site that helps with insurance claim jobs specifically?

Yes. That means a dedicated section explaining your insurance process, what a homeowner can expect working with their adjuster, and how to reach you for a same-day inspection. We include that if insurance work is part of what you want more of. It's one of the most effective things a roofing site can have during storm season.
