                What you get
                
What hand-coded actually means for your site

                

                    Most web agencies hand you a WordPress theme and call it a day. We don't. Every site we build starts as a blank file — which means faster load times, fewer security headaches, and no bloat you didn't ask for.

    Fast load times
    
                    
    
                        We consistently hit 90+ on PageSpeed Insights. Most of our sites load in under a second. That's not a trick — it's what happens when there's no unnecessary code.

    Actually secure

                    Why it matters
                    
Speed isn't a feature. It's the baseline.

                    

                        Google uses load time as a ranking signal. Visitors bounce from slow sites. Ads perform worse when landing pages are sluggish. A fast site doesn't guarantee anything — but a slow one costs you every day.
                    

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            
    
                                
                            
    
                            
    Every second of delay costs visitors. Studies consistently show higher bounce rates after 3 seconds. We build under 1.
    
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            
    
                                
                            
    
                            
    Core Web Vitals are a real ranking factor. A clean PageSpeed score doesn't guarantee first page — but it stops speed from working against you.
    
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            
    
                                
                            
    
                            
    If you're spending money on Google Ads, your Quality Score is tied to landing page experience. A faster site means you're not paying extra for the same clicks.

                How it works
                
Six steps. Four to six weeks. No surprises.

                

                    Here's exactly what happens from first call to launch — and what we need from you along the way.
                

                

                    Most projects go live in 4–6 weeks. The main variable is how quickly you can pull together photos, content, and business details. The faster you move, the faster we do.
                

            

            
    
                
  • 
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    1. We talk through what you need
    
                    
    
                        A call, not a questionnaire. Tell us what you're building, who it's for, and what's not working with your current setup. Usually 30–45 minutes.
                    
    
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    2. We send a proposal. You decide.
    
                    
    
                        After the call, we put together a scope and price. No pressure, no expiring offers. If it makes sense, we move forward. If not, no hard feelings.
                    
    
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    3. We design the site
    
                    
    
                        We build a full design mockup — desktop and mobile — based on everything we talked about. This is where the layout and visual direction take shape.
                    
    
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    4. You review and approve
    
                    
    
                        We walk you through it on a screen share. You tell us what to change. One round of revisions is standard. We don't write a line of code until you sign off.
                    
    
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    5. We build it
    
                    
    
                        Once the design is approved, we hand-code the full site — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, from scratch. No WordPress. No page builders. No shortcuts.
                    
    
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
    
                        
                        
                    
    
                    
    6. We launch it. Then we stay.
    
                    
    
                        We handle hosting, push the site live, and stay on as your team. Updates, new pages, performance monitoring — it's all included in the monthly subscription.

                Common questions
                
What people usually ask before booking

            

            

                

                    
                        Do you work with businesses outside Philadelphia?
                        
                    
                    
Yes. Most of our process is remote — the call, the design review, everything. We've built sites for businesses in Lancaster, PA and beyond. Location doesn't really matter for what we do.

                

                

                    
                        Why subscription pricing instead of a one-time build fee?
                        
                    
                    
Because a website isn't a one-time thing. Hosting, updates, new pages, performance monitoring — that's ongoing work. $150/mo covers all of it. No surprise invoices when something needs to change.

                

                

                    
                        What do I need to provide?
                        
                    
                    
Photos, your services and pricing, and a clear sense of who your customers are. We handle the copy unless you'd rather write it yourself. The more you can give us upfront, the faster we move.

                

                

                    
                        How long does it actually take?
                        
                    
                    
4–6 weeks for most projects. The main variable is how quickly you can get us photos and content. If you're ready to move fast, so are we.

                

                

                    
                        What's the difference between hand-coded and WordPress?
                        
                    
                    
WordPress runs on a database and a stack of plugins. Hand-coded sites are just files — HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Fewer moving parts means faster load times, fewer security holes, and no maintenance overhead. The tradeoff is you can't edit the site yourself the way you would in WordPress. That's what the subscription is for.

                

                

                    
                        Do you guarantee rankings?
                        
                    
                    
No. Anyone who does is lying. We build sites that give Google nothing to complain about — fast, clean code, solid on-page structure. What you actually rank for depends on your market, your content, and time. We'll be straight with you about what's realistic.
